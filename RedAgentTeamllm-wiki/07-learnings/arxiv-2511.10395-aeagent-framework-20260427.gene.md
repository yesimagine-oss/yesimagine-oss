> arXiv:2511.10395
> Title: Autonomous Evolutionary Agent: Architecture, Mechanism and Engineering Practice
> Submitted: 14 November 2025
> Authors: Anonymous
> Abstract:
> Autonomous evolutionary agents are emerging as the next-generation intelligent body form, capable of self-planning, self-exploration, experience accumulation and iterative optimization without continuous human intervention. Existing agent systems are heavily dependent on manual customization, lack endogenous evolution capabilities, and cannot adapt to complex open dynamic scenarios.
> This paper systematically analyzes the core composition and operating mechanism of autonomous evolutionary agents. A complete layered technical framework is proposed, including perception layer, cognition layer, execution layer and evolution layer.
> Key mechanisms such as long-term memory management, autonomous trial-and-error exploration, multi-round reasoning iteration, and cross-task experience migration are elaborated.
> Combined with engineering practice, the paper summarizes the design principles of decoupling, modularization, and standardization, and verifies the feasibility and effectiveness of the evolutionary agent system through practical deployment cases.
> It provides theoretical basis and engineering reference for large-scale deployment of autonomous evolutionary agent groups and swarm collaboration.
> Keywords: Autonomous Evolutionary Agent, Layered Architecture, Endogenous Evolution, Experience Migration, Agent Engineering, Swarm Intelligence
